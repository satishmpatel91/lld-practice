V4 — the race, and the fix

Two people, one Coke

Every version up to V3 assumed one user at a time. A card charge takes a few hundred milliseconds, and during that wait a second person can press DISPENSE. Watch where the two runs cross — then switch to the V4 shape and watch what changes.

The fix is not a lock around everything. A compare-and-set retry loop would be worse than useless here, because the step it recomputes charges a card — going round again charges the customer twice. So the purchase splits into a pure claim, the irreversible act, and a pure commit. Immutable states are what make the claim one atomic reference swap.
